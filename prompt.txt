MEMORIA'26 — CINEMATIC INTERACTIVE TICKETING WEBSITE
MASTER IMPLEMENTATION PROMPT FOR GOOGLE ANTIGRAVITY

Build a complete, production-quality, immersive event ticketing website for Memoria'26 — The Eclipse of Memories.

This is NOT a normal landing page with decorative animations.

The website must feel like an interactive theatrical experience.

The visitor should feel as though they are entering an empty concert stage, watching the stage come alive, discovering the eclipse, and then entering the Memoria'26 event.

Do NOT simplify the concept into basic fade-ins, slide-ins, floating cards, or generic parallax.

The opening experience is the most important part of the website.

1. CORE CREATIVE DIRECTION

The visual concept is:

AN EMPTY STAGE → LIGHT → MEMORY → ECLIPSE → MEMORIA'26 → EVENT

The website should visually communicate:

mystery
anticipation
theatrical atmosphere
elegance
nostalgia
music
moonlight
memories
an eclipse
premium concert/event production

The visitor should feel:

"Something is about to begin."

Then, through scrolling:

"The stage is coming alive."

Then:

"This is Memoria'26."

The animation must be driven by the user's scroll position wherever appropriate.

Do NOT make the entire experience autoplay and finish before the visitor interacts with it.

Scrolling must be part of the storytelling.

9. SCENE 02 — THE VISITOR STARTS SCROLLING

The animation must react to scroll progress.

When the visitor begins scrolling:

The camera should slowly move toward the stage.

At the same time:

LEFT SPOTLIGHT:

rotate slowly toward center.

RIGHT SPOTLIGHT:

rotate slowly toward center.

The two beams gradually converge.

Do not make this fast.

It must feel cinematic.

Use smooth interpolation/spring behavior.

Avoid sudden jumps.

The stage should gradually become visible.

10. SCENE 03 — LIGHT REVEALS THE ATMOSPHERE

move vertically

13. SCENE 06 — THE ECLIPSE

This is the central visual moment.

The moon slowly transitions into an eclipse.

Do NOT simply fade one image into another.

Create the feeling of an eclipse:

Moon becomes brighter.
Dark circular shadow begins crossing the moon.
Shadow gradually covers the moon.
A glowing rim remains visible.
The surrounding atmosphere develops a subtle purple/magenta bloom.
The stage remains illuminated underneath.
The eclipse stabilizes.

The eclipse should feel mysterious and elegant.

Avoid cheesy sci-fi effects.

This is a musical/theatrical event, not a space game.
